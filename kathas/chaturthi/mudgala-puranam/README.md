# kathas/chaturthi/mudgala-puranam/

- `chaturthi-vrata-varnanam.tex` — from the Mudgala Purāṇa: Śaunaka questions Sūta on the chaturthī vrata via Dakṣa and Mudgala's dialogue. The first six chapters (अध्याय) lay out the vrata's general rules and merit; the remaining chapters then go month by month through the year's śukla- and kṛṣṇa-pakṣa chaturthī māhātmyas, from Bhādrapada through Māgha kṛṣṇa (twenty chapters in all).
