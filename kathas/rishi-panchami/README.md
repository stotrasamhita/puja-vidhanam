# kathas/rishi-panchami/

- `rishi-panchami-vrata-katha.tex` — the Ṛṣipañcamī vrata katha from the Bhaviṣyottara Purāṇa (as preserved in the Vrataratnakara): the story of Sushīlā's daughter, and of Sumati's parents reborn as a dog and a bull for touching things while menstruating, redeemed by observing Ṛṣipañcamī — the vrata of worshipping the Saptarṣis and Arundhatī to atone for such inadvertent ritual lapses.
