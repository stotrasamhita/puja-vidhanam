# kathas/amavasya-somavara-vratam/

- `amavasya-somavara-vrata-katha.tex` — the katha for the Somavatī-Amāvāsyā vrata (worship of Aśvattha-Nārāyaṇa — Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Śiva in the peepal tree — on an Amāvāsyā that falls on a Monday), from the Bhaviṣyottara Purāṇa's Bhīṣma-Yudhiṣṭhira saṃvāda: Bhīṣma, on his bed of arrows, teaches Yudhiṣṭhira this vrata for progeny and longevity, illustrating it with the story of Somā the washerwoman, and closing with the vrata's full vidhi and udyāpana as he recounts them.
